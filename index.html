Microporous Technology

Kobrapor Microporous Technology is an engineered solution applied to the manufacturing of high-performance porous polymers.

It is especially used in the development of flame arrestors and battery vents for lead-acid batteries, delivering protection and control at the microscopic level.

How It Works

Controlled flow.
Proven protection.

Installed on the battery vent, the Kobrapor flame arrestor acts as a smart barrier: it ensures safe venting of internal gases and completely blocks external flames or sparks from entering.

Blocks Flame Propagation

The microporous structure stops the spread of flames and sparks, preventing external ignition from reaching the inside of the battery.

Controls Gas Flow

The engineered pores allow controlled release of internally generated gases, keeping pressure within safe operating limits.

Balances Internal Pressure

The microporous vent acts as a pressure-equalizing valve, extending battery life and preventing structural damage from overpressure.

Shapes & Applications

Precise geometries.
Controlled flows.

Kobrapor flame arrestors are supplied in the battery industry's standard shapes. Custom geometries are developed to fit each specific application.

Shape

Circular

Standard shape for automotive battery vents. Available in multiple diameters per customer specification.

  • High chemical resistance
  • Calibrated gas flow
  • Compatible with PE, PP and PTFE
Shape

Rectangular

Ideal for prismatic and stationary storage batteries. Provides more flow area per installed unit.

  • Larger flow surface area
  • Snap-fit or adhesive installation
  • Precise dimensional tolerances
Shape

Oblong

Capsule shape for elongated cavities. Combines high flow area with easy placement in automated assembly.

  • Perfect for automated assembly
  • Ergonomic insertion geometry
  • Standardizable corner radius
Custom

  • Joint development with the customer
  • Rapid prototyping available
